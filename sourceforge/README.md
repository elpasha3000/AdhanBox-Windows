# 🕌 AdhanBox for Windows

**The adhan, on time, on your PC — free, offline, open source.**

AdhanBox is a small native Windows application that plays the **adhan** at every prayer time and shows today's prayer times: Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha — with a live clock and a countdown to the next prayer.

Prayer times are **calculated on your own computer** from your city's coordinates using the classical astronomical method. The app has **no network code at all** — it works with no Internet connection, sends nothing anywhere, and shows no ads.

## ✨ Features

- **Plays the adhan automatically** at every prayer time — the Makkah adhan is built in, or add your own MP3 files
- **264 cities in 67 countries** built in · **20 calculation methods** (Egypt, Umm al-Qura, MWL, ISNA, Karachi, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Tehran…) · Standard or Hanafi Asr
- **Per prayer:** its own sound, its own volume, its own play duration (full adhan or N seconds), a ± minute offset, an on/off switch, and a test button
- **Global time shift** for all prayers at once, with an Apply button
- **Mute for 1 hour or 24 hours** from the tray icon
- **Notification 10 minutes before** each adhan
- **Starts with Windows minimized** — the window appears by itself at adhan time and hides again when the adhan ends
- **Live clock** and countdown to the next prayer; after the last prayer it counts down to tomorrow's Fajr
- **Complete Arabic and English interfaces** — the Arabic layout is properly mirrored right-to-left
- **Native C++** — a single small exe, a few MB of RAM, no .NET, no Electron, no background services
